Excess proceeds, tax foreclosure

When a Texas county sells a property at tax foreclosure for more than the taxes owed, the difference goes into the registry of the court in the former owner's name. The county publishes it and then waits. Most owners are never told, and the money sits for years until a claim is filed.

Why the wait costs you

Counties move surplus to the state, apply it to other liens, or pay it to whoever files first. Every sale season adds another name to the register. The claim does not get easier with time.
