Why the wait costs you

Counties move surplus to the state, apply it against other liens, or pay it to whoever files first. Every sale season adds another name to the register. The claim does not get easier with time, and some states run a short clock on the deposit.

The boundary

We prepare and file paperwork. We are not lawyers and this is not legal advice. The clerk's own ledger controls the amount. If the file needs a hearing we hand it to you and stay on the clock.

Three questions everyone asks

Is the money real? The register is published by the county, not by us. Before you pay, search the case number on the county's own site or ring the clerk named on this page. Nothing here depends on taking our word for it.

Why pay before the claim? The search and the file are the work. After that the money is in the clerk's hands, not ours, and you send it in yourself, or we file it for you.

What if someone already claimed it? The clerk would have paid it and it comes off the register. You get the answer inside four hours and the fee back if the line is gone.
